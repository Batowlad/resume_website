STS AI

In progress · Jun 2026 – Present

An LLM agent that learns to play Slay the Spire. I wrapped the C++17 sts_lightspeed engine with pybind11 and turned its objects into 14 frozen dataclasses, so observations, rollout logs and reward shaping all share one typed representation. The draw pile's order is hidden from the prompt, so the policy never trains on information it won't have at inference. Actions are logged as position-independent keys, so rollouts stay replayable across engine versions. Next up: supervised fine-tuning and GRPO with TRL.

Mood-Max

Chrome extension · 5 stars · Sep 2025 – Present

Reads the article you're on and plays mood-matched background music, with no account or paid streaming subscription. A 3-node LangGraph pipeline extracts theme and mood, generates tags and picks tracks, with every LLM call bound to a Pydantic schema. An Express backend resolves the tracks on YouTube, caches them, and keeps the API keys off the client.
